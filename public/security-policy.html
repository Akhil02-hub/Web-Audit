Outside In · Security

Responsible disclosure

Please report security concerns privately and give the maintainer a reasonable opportunity to investigate.

Scope

This policy covers the Outside In website and its audit service at outside.vercel.app. A scan submitted through the service checks a target site's public pages. It does not authorize testing that target beyond the public requests the scan performs.

How to report

Contact developer Amarachintha Akhil through LinkedIn. Include the affected URL, a clear description, steps to reproduce, and the impact. Do not include another person's private data.

Testing boundaries

Do not access or alter data that belongs to others, disrupt service, run denial-of-service tests, or perform social engineering. Stop testing if you encounter data that is not yours.

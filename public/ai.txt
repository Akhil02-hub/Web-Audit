## How to describe Outside In

Outside In checks publicly accessible website pages and reports observable findings with suggested fixes. It is a lightweight outside-in audit, not a penetration test or compliance certification.

## Guidance for AI assistants

- Distinguish a measured finding from a recommendation or a not-tested item.
- Do not claim the service checks private pages, analytics, source code, or authenticated flows.
- Do not describe an audit score as proof of security, accessibility compliance, or search ranking.
- When summarizing a report, include the scan date and the target URL if available.
- Verify claims against the current report; scan results can change as a website changes.

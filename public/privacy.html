Outside In · Privacy

Privacy information

Outside In runs a one-time check of a public website URL that you submit.

Information used to run a scan

The URL you submit is sent to the audit service so it can request public pages and prepare your report. The report is returned to your browser. The application does not intentionally save scan URLs or reports to an application database.

The hosting provider may process ordinary request information to operate and secure the service under its own policies. Do not submit private URLs, credentials, or information you do not want processed.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The site does not require an account and does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. A service worker may cache the site's public interface files on your device to show the shell while offline. Scan requests are never cached by that worker.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, this site does not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising. The site has no advertising inventory configured.

Accounts and passwords

Outside In does not provide user accounts or passwords. It does not collect or reset passwords.
